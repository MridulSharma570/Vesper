# Frequently Asked Questions

> How Vesper handles anonymity, recovery, encryption, abuse reports and platforms — answered plainly.

*Operator: Mridul Sharma · Contact: vesper.privacy@proton.me*

## Do I need a phone number or email to sign up?

No. Vesper creates a pseudonymous account with a random handle and identity key. You can link an email or phone later for recovery, but it stays optional forever and is stored only as a hash.

## Can people I chat with see who I am?

Only what you choose to show: your display name, handle and avatar. Your linked contacts, device details and IP address are never exposed to other users.

## What happens if I lose my device?

If you linked an email or phone, you can recover the account with a verification code. If you did not, the account cannot be recovered — that is the trade-off of not storing any identity about you. We will never ask you for ID documents.

## Is Vesper end-to-end encrypted?

Messages are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on the server. A true end-to-end encryption mode exists as an operator-controlled experimental flag and is documented honestly on the Encryption page, including what is not finished yet.

## Can I change my handle or disappear?

Yes. Rotate your handle from Settings whenever you like; old handles are released, not forwarded. Deleting your account removes your content within one sweep cycle and the remaining rows within 30 days.

## Does Vesper show ads or sell data?

No ads, no data sales, no brokers, no ad SDKs. The business model is the operator’s own; your attention and your data are not the product.

## Which platforms are supported?

One codebase ships to the web, Android, iOS and Windows from the same source tree, so behaviour and security properties stay identical everywhere.

## How do I report abuse?

In-app: long-press a message or open a profile and choose Report. Without an account: the public report form on this site. Both paths reach the same moderated queue, and statutory categories (CSAE, self-harm) jump it.

## What are people saying about Vesper?

We publish testimonials only from real users who explicitly agree to be quoted, and none have been collected yet — so you will not see any on this site. When real ones exist, they will appear here with permission, unedited.

## Who runs Vesper?

Vesper is operated by Mridul Sharma. Policy questions go to vesper.privacy@proton.me; security reports are triaged ahead of feature work.
